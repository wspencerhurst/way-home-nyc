1 / 4 · The problem

On a crowd night, the streets change every minute, and nobody has the whole picture.

ImprovisedNYPD closes streets as crowds move. There's no full list, and DOT's advisories leave out emergency closures.
ScatteredThe facts are split across Notify NYC, MTA alerts, X, Instagram, Reddit and 311.
On footPeople are walking, on jammed cell networks. Driving apps can't see barricades or closed subway entrances.

3 / 4 · How routes change live

Crowd reports reroute people within minutes, and every item shows how trustworthy it is.

Unverified1–2 reportsShown on the map as a warning. Routes don't change yet.
Confirmed3+ independent reports from 2+ platforms within 10 minRoutes start avoiding it automatically. This is the crowd-sourced reroute.
OfficialNYPD, DOT or MTA, or one operator clickTreated as a hard closure. Sent to Notify NYC and map apps.

Five checks on every post: time (after the final buzzer?) · place (does the photo match the claimed street?) · media (recycled from the June parade? AI-made?) · source (part of a bot burst?) · corroboration. Reports fade out after 20 minutes. Crowd data can only steer people away from a street, never toward one.
